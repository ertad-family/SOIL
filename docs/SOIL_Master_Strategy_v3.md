## 1. Vision & Mission

### 1.1 The Grand Vision

SOIL is a research-first project devoted to collecting organizational autopsy data at scale to ignite a completely new scientific field: **Organizational Biology, Health, and Medicine**.

Just as pioneers centuries ago began systematically documenting human deaths and conducting autopsies — eventually giving rise to modern medicine, pathology, and healthcare — SOIL's mission is to do exactly the same for organizations.

We stand at the beginning of a new discipline. Organizations are born, grow, get sick, and die — yet we have no systematic understanding of why. No taxonomy of organizational diseases. No diagnostic frameworks. No preventive medicine. No treatment protocols.

### 1.2 Mission Statement

*"Transform organizational failure from wasted potential into collective wisdom."*

### 1.3 Why This Matters

- **90% of startups fail**, but we don't understand why systematically
- **Trillions of dollars** and countless human years are wasted on preventable organizational deaths
- **Existing knowledge** is anecdotal, biased, and unsearchable
- **No scientific discipline** treats organizations as living systems worthy of medical-style study
- **Future generations** of founders deserve evidence-based guidance, not folklore

### 1.4 The Opportunity

By creating the infrastructure for organizational medicine, SOIL positions itself to:

- Define an entirely new scientific field
- Build irreplaceable datasets with compound value over decades
- Spawn multiple derivative institutions (education, diagnostics, treatment)
- Fundamentally improve how humanity builds and maintains organizations

## 2. Scientific Foundation

### 2.1 The Biological Analogy

Organizations face the same fundamental challenges as living organisms:

| Challenge | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Multi-dimensional uncertainty** | Organizations cannot predict the future any more than organisms can predict environmental changes |
| **Asynchronous feedback** | Months pass between action and result — like evolutionary adaptations taking generations to prove successful |
| **Interdependence** | Every system affects every other system; optimization of one impacts others |
| **Coordination complexity** | Managing multiple actors with different interests is fundamentally difficult |

These aren't just analogies — they're **isomorphic problems**. Both organizations and organisms are complex adaptive systems trying to survive in uncertain environments by coordinating distributed components.

> **Origin:** The full intellectual journey from "why is business hard?" to "let's build a startup cemetery" is documented in [requiem_genesis_story.md](./requiem_genesis_story.md).

### 2.2 The Research Gap

**What biology has:**
- Taxonomy of organism types
- Understanding of required systems
- Knowledge of failure modes
- Predictive models for survival
- Centuries of autopsy data

**What organizational science lacks:**
- Systematic classification of organization types
- Structured analysis of why organizations die
- Comprehensive failure databases
- Predictive frameworks
- Any significant autopsy data

### 2.3 Framework-Agnostic Approach

> **Critical Design Decision:** SOIL does NOT commit to any single analytical framework.

Instead, SOIL:

1. **Collects data in neutral formats** — capturing organizational state without imposing theoretical structure
2. **Applies multiple frameworks post-hoc** — testing existing theories against real data
3. **Lets patterns emerge** — allowing data to reveal truth rather than confirming preconceptions
4. **Compares predictive power** — determining which frameworks best explain and predict organizational mortality

**Frameworks to be applied include (but are not limited to):**

- Biological 13-system metaphor (see [SOIL_Strategy_Harmonized_v2.md](./SOIL_Strategy_Harmonized_v2.md), Section 9.2)
- Traditional business frameworks (Porter, McKinsey 7S, etc.)
- Organizational behavior models
- Systems thinking frameworks
- Complexity theory approaches
- Industry-specific analytical models
- Lean Startup / Agile frameworks

This approach removes initial bias, tests existing theories against real data, and builds credibility through methodological rigor.

## 4. The Cenotaphery

The cenotaphery is the foundational data intake mechanism — a dignified virtual space where founders memorialize their failed organizations while contributing structured data to the research mission.

### 4.1 Purpose

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Data Collection** | Systematic capture of organizational autopsy data across analytical frameworks |
| **Therapeutic Function** | Dignified closure for founders; ritual processing of organizational death |
| **Community Building** | Connects founders with shared experiences; builds contributor base |
| **Aesthetic Experience** | Beautiful 3D virtual memorial space; respects the effort founders invested |

### 4.2 Geographic Structure

Cenotapheries are organized geographically following a hierarchical system:

| Level | Trigger | Capacity | Naming |
|-------|---------|----------|--------|
| **Country** | Initial | 1,024 cenotaphs | "Federal Cenotaphery of [Country]" |
| **Regional** | Country hits capacity | 1,024 each | "State Cenotaphery of [Region]" |
| **City** | Region hits capacity | 1,024 each | "[City] Cenotaphery" |

**Cenotaphery Naming Honor:** When a cenotaphery reaches capacity, it gains a permanent honorific: "In the name of [Most Prominent Cenotaph]" — determined by the most-visited cenotaph at the moment of closure, frozen forever.

### 4.3 Cenotaph Visual Design

Each cenotaph features custom AI-generated artwork unique to that organization:

1. **During creation:** Founder collaborates with AI on 2D prototype
2. **Iterative refinement:** Until founder approves design
3. **3D generation:** AI generates corresponding 3D model with local geographic nuances
4. **Placement:** Final 3D cenotaph placed in cenotaphery

**Key principle:** Monument design is **permanent** — it becomes part of the cenotaph's identity. First design is free; redesign is a premium paid service.

### 4.4 Visual Aging System (Replaces Decay)

> **Critical Change from v2:** Cenotaphs **never disappear or become inaccessible**. The decay mechanism has been replaced with visual aging.

**How aging works:**

| Aspect | Mechanism |
|--------|-----------|
| **Aging progression** | Every day, cenotaph age increases by 1 |
| **Visual manifestation** | Weathering, patina, moss/lichen growth, overgrown vegetation, fallen leaves, dust |
| **Natural slowing** | Visits slow down visible aging (community "maintenance") |
| **Restoration** | Founders can spend Respects to restore visual freshness |
| **Permanence** | The cenotaph never disappears — it may weather, but it endures |

**Visual aging stages:**

| Stage | Time | Visual Signs |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Fresh | 0-30 days | Pristine, as designed |
| Settled | 30-180 days | Slight patina, natural settling |
| Mature | 180-365 days | Dignified weathering, some moss |
| Aged | 1-3 years | Significant weathering, vegetation growth |
| Ancient | 3+ years | Heavy aging, possible overgrowth, "ruins" aesthetic |

> **Full aging and restoration mechanics:** See [SOIL_Economic_Model_v2.md](./SOIL_Economic_Model_v2.md), Section 4.2

### 4.5 Navigation & Discovery

The 3D cenotaphery environment is complemented by a feed-based interface for accessibility:

**3D Cenotaphery:** Immersive desktop experience, spatial exploration

**Feed Interface (TikTok-style):** Mobile-optimized, vertical swipe interface featuring:
- Sequential browsing through cenotaphery by address
- Search/filter by industry, geography, time period, failure type, funding stage, team size
- Discovery feeds: Most prominent, regional highlights, recently added, rising, personalized

### 4.6 Cenotaph Structure

Each organizational cenotaph contains:

| Layer | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Basic Information** | Name, dates, industry, geography, funding stage, team size, description |
| **In-Depth Analysis** | Multi-framework assessment, failure timeline, primary cause + cascades, early warning signs, quantitative metrics |
| **Narrative Elements** | AI-generated narrative (facts-based), founder's lessons learned, custom 3D visual memorial |
| **Optional Features** | Crypt storage (documents, code, media), private visibility settings |

## 5. Economic Model

> **Full specification:** [SOIL_Economic_Model_v2.md](./SOIL_Economic_Model_v2.md)

### 5.1 Design Philosophy

The economic model was **radically simplified** from the original dual-currency system:

| Removed | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|
| Days as separate currency | Cognitive overload for grieving founders |
| Decay = death mechanism | Contradicts "every ending deserves dignity" |
| Floating exchange rates | Unpredictable value confuses users |
| Regional micro-economies | Implementation complexity without clear benefit |

| Kept | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| Respects as single currency | Simple gamification that works |
| Earning through valuable actions | Aligns user behavior with platform goals |
| Spending on customization | Optional enhancement, not mandatory survival |

### 5.2 Single Currency: Respects

**Characteristics:**
- Fixed value: 1 Respect = 1 Respect (no exchange rates)
- Earned primarily through valuable platform actions
- Giftable to other cenotaphs (not your own)
- Non-expiring
- Account-bound (cannot be withdrawn)

**New user welcome bonus:** 10 Respects upon registration (can only gift to others)

### 5.3 Earning Summary

## 6. Data Collection & Interview System

> **Full specification:** [org_functions_matrix.md](./org_functions_matrix.md)

### 6.1 Temporal Anchor

All functional data is collected at **"Peak Operations"** — the moment when the organization was working at its best. This:
- Is easy for founders to identify
- Shows maximum capabilities
- Minimizes emotional bias from final crisis period

### 6.2 Module Structure

| Module | Method | Duration | Content |
|--------|--------|----------|---------|
| **0. Basic Info** | Form | 5 min | Organization basics, timeline, founder role |
| **1. Functional Mapping** | Point-and-click wizard | 30-40 min | All functions at peak + health check |
| **2. Financial Picture** | Upload + structured | 15-20 min | Financial metrics, dynamics, events |
| **3. Dynamic Picture** | Timeline builder | 20-30 min | Internal events peak → death |
| **4. Environment Analysis** | Structured + events | 15-20 min | Resources, external events |
| **5. Founder Context** | Structured + events | 15-20 min | Background, dynamics, toll, personal events |
| **6. Narrative** | Text / Interview | 20-30 min | Meaning, lessons, closure |

**Total self-service time:** ~2 hours  
**With human interview:** ~2.5-3 hours

### 6.3 Organization Type Adaptation

The interview adapts based on organization type:
- Tech Product (SaaS, apps, platforms)
- Services (agencies, consulting)
- E-commerce / Retail
- Manufacturing
- NGO / Non-profit
- Media / Content

Each type shows relevant functions and hides irrelevant ones.

### 6.4 Three-Layer Content Architecture

| Layer | Purpose | Visibility |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Raw Intake** | Therapeutic founder expression + rich data | Never public; encrypted |
| **AI-Refined Facts** | Clean research dataset | Anonymized; searchable; aggregatable |
| **Curated Cenotaph** | Beautiful public artifact | Founder-approved; dignity-preserving |

**Publicity tiers (founder chooses):**
- Tier 1: Full anonymity (default) — pattern + data only
- Tier 2: Pseudonym + story — industry, geography, dates, narrative without names
- Tier 3: Full publicity (opt-in) — organization name, founder name, full story

## 8. Research Center

The Research Center is the scientific heart of SOIL — transforming raw autopsy data into actionable knowledge and predictive capabilities.

### 8.1 Mission

To conduct rigorous, unbiased research on organizational mortality, identify patterns and causes of organizational death, and develop the scientific foundation for organizational medicine.

### 8.2 Core Activities

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Data Analysis** | Statistical analysis, cross-framework studies, industry-specific mortality research |
| **Pattern Recognition** | Failure archetypes, early warning signals, cascade failure analysis |
| **AI/ML Development** | Training proprietary LLM, predictive models, diagnostic algorithms |
| **Academic Publishing** | Peer-reviewed papers, conference presentations, open dataset releases |

### 8.3 Research Principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Agnosticism** | No predetermined conclusions; multiple frameworks tested equally |
| **Rigor** | Academic standards; peer review; transparent limitations |
| **Ethics** | Anonymization by default; founder consent respected; no harmful applications |

### 8.4 Outputs

**Near-term:**
- Annual "State of Organizational Mortality" report
- Industry-specific failure pattern analyses
- Framework validation studies
- Predictive model prototypes

**Long-term:**
- Proprietary organizational health LLM
- Diagnostic assessment tools
- Treatment protocol recommendations
- Textbooks and educational materials

## 11. Development Ecosystem

> **Full specification:** [SOIL_Development_Ecosystem_Strategy.md](./SOIL_Development_Ecosystem_Strategy.md)

### 11.1 Core Strategic Model

SOIL adopts a "GitLab-style" approach: open source code with centralized deployment.

| Component | Strategy |
|-----------|----------|
| **Code** | Open source (MIT License) — community can contribute, fork, learn |
| **Deployment** | Centralized on soil.rip — single canonical instance |
| **Data** | Proprietary — the irreplaceable moat |
| **AI Models** | Proprietary — trained on unique dataset |
| **Brand** | Trademarked — "SOIL" protected |

### 11.2 Why This Works

Forking code produces an empty cemetery with:
- No data
- No network effects
- No verification system
- No brand trust

The value is in the ecosystem, not the code.

### 11.3 Keeper Economics

Keepers are local operators who drive SOIL's global expansion — franchise partners with aligned economic incentives.

**Revenue streams:**
- Transaction share (20-30% of purchases in their region)
- Pathologist fees (80% of interview fees)
- Event revenue (70% of sponsorships)
- Referral bonuses ($5-10 per verified cenotaph)

**Example income (Lagos Keeper, Year 2):** ~$500/month

| First Contribution | Small fix, PR merged, listed in Contributors |
| Regular Contributor | Multiple PRs, "Contributor" badge |

## 13. Network Effects & Growth

### 13.1 Primary Network Effect: Marketplace Discovery

**Founder Journey (seeking help):**
1. Google searches specific failure scenario
2. Finds matching cenotaph via SEO
3. Reads full content (open for SEO)
4. Clicks "Contact Founder" (registration required)
5. Connects with experienced founder

**Network Density Effect:**
More cenotaphs → better matching → more consultations → more testimonials → more cenotaphs

### 13.2 Data Network Effects

| Threshold | Unlock |
|-----------|--------|
| 500 cenotaphs | Patterns emerge |
| 2,000 cenotaphs | Statistical significance, benchmarking |
| 5,000 cenotaphs | Deep industry patterns, predictive power |
| 10,000+ cenotaphs | AI-powered prevention, field dominance |

### 13.3 Viral Mechanics: Research Model

**Adopted approach:**
- Research contribution as status
- Pattern recognition as value
- Anonymous data as product
- Scientific credibility as brand

**Rejected approach:**
- Drama-based virality
- Named failures as clickbait
- Exploitation of founder pain

### 13.4 SEO & Discovery Strategy

Each cenotaph = landing page for hyper-specific searches.

**Open Access for SEO:** Complete content visible to search engines

## 14. Verification & Trust

### 14.1 Verification Requirements

**Minimum definition of "Organization":**
- At least 2 people involved at some point (co-founder, employee, contractor)
- OR external stakeholders (paying customers, investors, formal suppliers)
- Solo side projects explicitly excluded

**Verification threshold:**
- 3 confirmations from ex-colleagues, ex-customers, ex-suppliers, or ex-partners
- Plus optional document upload (incorporation papers, dissolution documents)
- Cenotaph remains private/draft until verification achieved

### 14.2 Verification Flow

1. Founder provides 5-10 contact emails with relationships
2. Platform sends automated verification requests
3. Verifiers click link → confirm relationship → done (30 seconds)
4. Post-verification: "Have you experienced organizational closure? Create your cenotaph"
5. After 3 verifications → cenotaph goes public

### 14.3 Regional Verification Flexibility

| Region | Primary Signal | Alternative Signals |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| US/UK/Canada | LinkedIn profile | Professional email domain |
| Germany/Austria | LinkedIn or Xing | Professional email, company registry |
| Russia/CIS | Professional email | VK profile, government registry |
| China | WeChat Work | Maimai profile, company registry |
| LATAM | LinkedIn partial | WhatsApp Business, local registries |
| Africa | Professional email | Phone verification + documents |

### 14.4 Co-Founder Considerations

**Core Principle:** You own your story. You don't own other people's identities.

**What proceeds regardless of objection:**
- Organization name, dates, industry, what it did
- Your personal narrative and learnings
- Framework analysis from YOUR perspective
- Anonymized data contribution

**What requires consent:**
- Naming other co-founders by name
- Specific characterizations of individuals
